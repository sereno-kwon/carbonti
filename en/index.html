01

Brand

CARBON-TICarbon · Titanium · Italy

Two materials,
one component.
Less weight, more stiffness.

CARBON-TI is an Italian bicycle component brand that, as the name says, works in carbon and titanium. By combining the strengths of different materials in a single part, it takes weight out and adds stiffness and durability wherever a component spins, brakes or holds.

From the drivetrain and brakes that riders feel most, like chainrings and disc rotors, to thru axles, headset caps and a single bolt. CARBON-TI believes the difference in material and machining shows even in the smallest part.

01 · Carbon

Carbon body

02 · Steel & Titanium

Metal only where it meets

The teeth the chain engages, the surface the pads grip, the bolts that take the load. Metal goes only where friction and load demand it.

Chainring

X-CARBORINGX-CARBORING EVO · X-SINGLECARBON · X-AERORING

The biggest part of the crank,
made in carbon.

X-CARBORING pairs a carbon chainring body with metal teeth where the chain engages. It lightens the largest, heaviest part of the crank while keeping the stiffness that shifting and power transfer need.

EVO for outer rings, X-SINGLECARBON for 1x drivetrains and X-AERORING for Dura-Ace 9200. Pick the crank fitment and tooth count to match your drivetrain.

03

Disc Rotor

X-ROTORSTEELCARBON 3 · AERO

Steel where it brakes,
carbon where it carries.

X-ROTOR STEELCARBON 3 is a hybrid disc rotor with a steel outer ring where the pads bite and a carbon carrier joining it to the hub. It keeps the metal that braking needs and takes out spinning weight.

X-ROTOR AERO refines the same idea for racing. Both Center Lock and 6 bolt mounts are available, along with X-AFS versions, adapters, lock rings and a titanium bolt kit sold separately.
